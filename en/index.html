    Build a team lineup in minutes

    Squadraw is a free tool to plan and share your football or rugby team lineup. Everything happens in your browser: no sign-up, no app to install, and your lineups stay on your device.

    Football lineups for 11-a-side

    Pick one of six formations (4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 3-4-3 and 5-3-2), then move your players anywhere on the pitch. Add up to 12 substitutes, goalkeepers included, and make changes by drag and drop.

    Rugby sevens, league and union lineups

    Each format starts from its own positions: props, hooker, second row, back row, scrum-half, fly-half, centres, wings and full-back. Shirt numbers stay editable and every format keeps its own team.

    Kits in your club colours

    Pick a ready-made kit or set the body, sleeve and detail colours yourself, with a plain, striped, hooped or sash pattern. Goalkeepers get their own kit colour.

    A shareable image in one click

    Export your team sheet as a high-resolution PNG with the pitch, starters and substitutes, ready for WhatsApp, social media or the dressing-room wall. You can also save the lineup as a file to open it again later.

    How does it work?

    1. Choose the sport and format: 11-a-side football, or rugby sevens, league or union.
    2. Name your team and enter your players, or paste a list of names.
    3. Drag the shirts on the pitch to fine-tune positions.
    4. Export the image and share the lineup with your team.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Squadraw free?

    Yes, Squadraw is completely free and needs no account.

    Where are my lineups saved?

    They are saved automatically in your browser, on your device, and are never sent to a server. To keep or move them, use “Save file” to download a backup.

    Can I use Squadraw on my phone?

    Yes. The site adapts to phones and tablets, and you can drag players with your finger.

    Which rugby formats are available?

    Rugby sevens, rugby league (13) and rugby union (15), each with its own starting positions and saved lineup.

    How do I share my lineup?

    Click “Export image” to get a PNG with the pitch and substitutes, ready to send on WhatsApp, Instagram or by email.
